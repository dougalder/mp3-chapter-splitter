    Cuts land on MPEG frame boundaries, so each split is within ~26 ms of its marker and the audio is copied bit-for-bit — no re-encoding, no generation loss. As with any frame-accurate cut, the first fraction of a second of a track can carry a faint artifact where the encoder's bit reservoir was left behind; the same is true of ffmpeg -c copy.
